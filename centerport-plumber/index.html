What to expect from a plumbing visit

The work depends on the issue. A plumber may assess the affected fixture or plumbing connection, discuss what was found, and carry out a repair or installation suited to the job. Give a clear description rather than guessing at the cause. If you know when the trouble began or what changed beforehand, share that too.

Before the visit, consider this checklist:

  • Identify the affected room, fixture, or area.
  • Note when the issue started and whether it has changed.
  • Move stored items away from the work area if you can do so safely.
  • Keep track of any questions about the proposed work.

Avoid taking apart a fixture or trying a repair you aren't comfortable doing. A temporary attempt can make it harder to see the original issue. If the problem involves active water damage, call for help rather than waiting to see if it clears up.
